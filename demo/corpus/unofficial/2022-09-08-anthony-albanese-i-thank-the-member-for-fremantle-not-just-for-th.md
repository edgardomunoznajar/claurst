I thank the member for Fremantle not just for the question but for his advocacy for climate change action. He has been a very strong advocate in his local electorate and around the country of the need for Australia to move forward into this century, because we've been held back. We know that, under the previous government, we saw four gigs out and one gig in when it came to energy production.
